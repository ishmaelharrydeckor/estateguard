How It Works

From Verified Home to Receipt in Five Steps

Phase 1 uses mobile-money or bank approval. Salary-linked payment is a later option.

01Verify and Approve

Tenant and landlord are identity-verified. The property is inspected and receives an EG Property ID, such as EG-ACC-000125.

02Sign Digitally

Both sides sign a digital tenancy agreement. Rent rules are checked first.

03Approve on Your Phone

Each month the tenant gets reminders, then approves a payment prompt on their phone.

04Collected and Split

The payment partner collects the money. The landlord's share goes to the landlord and EstateGuard's fee to EstateGuard. EstateGuard never holds the rent money.

05Receipt and Daily Check

A digital receipt is issued, and every payment is checked against the partner's records each day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about EstateGuard.

Ask a Question
Do you hold our money?

No. A licensed payment partner collects each payment and splits it. The landlord's share goes to the landlord and EstateGuard's fee goes to EstateGuard.

What if a tenant does not pay?

EstateGuard does not guarantee rent. Tenants get reminders each month before they approve a payment, and every payment is recorded with a digital receipt. What happens after a missed payment is set out in the tenancy agreement [to be confirmed with counsel].

How do you protect personal data?

We do not store ID documents or bank or wallet numbers. A tenant's rental record is shared only with their consent, and every action is kept in a tamper-evident audit trail.

Is this a government programme?

No. EstateGuard is a private platform. It follows government rent rules but does not create them. Any housing programme or certification (Phase 3) would happen only after a formal agreement.

What about salary deductions?

Not in Phase 1. Tenants approve each payment by mobile money or bank. Salary-linked payment is a later option and would need payroll and regulatory approvals.

What do you need from us?

Pilot partners, introductions to the right people, and the approvals that apply to your organisation. We can walk you through the details in a short briefing.
